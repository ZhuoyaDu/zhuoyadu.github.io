招生与联系Advising & Contact

如果你在考虑做公司金融、金融发展或数字化与创新方向的论文，欢迎来信。来信请附一段说明：你想弄清楚什么问题，以及为什么这个问题值得做。有 Stata 或 Python 实证经验会有帮助，但更重要的是你对问题本身有真实的兴趣。本科生的毕业论文选题咨询、数据与方法问题同样欢迎。

If you are thinking about a thesis in corporate finance, financial development, or digitalization and innovation, feel free to write. Please include a short note on the question you want to answer and why you think it is worth answering. Experience with Stata or Python helps, but genuine interest in the question matters more. Undergraduates are equally welcome to ask about thesis topics, data, and methods.
